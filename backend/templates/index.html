Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I save with an S-Corporation?

The savings depend on your net profit, but typically range from $2,000-$20,000+ per year for profitable businesses. Use our calculator to get a personalized estimate.

Is this tax advice?

No. This tool provides estimates only. Always consult with a qualified tax professional before making any business decisions.

What's an "S-Corporation"?

An S-Corp is a tax classification (not a business structure) that allows you to avoid 15.3% self-employment tax on dividends. You still need to pay reasonable W-2 wages, but the remainder can be taxed as dividends.
