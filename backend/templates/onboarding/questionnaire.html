How it Works

An S-Corporation allows you to reduce self-employment tax by:

  1. Paying yourself a "reasonable salary" as W-2 wages (subject to SE tax)
  2. Taking the remaining profit as dividends (NOT subject to SE tax)

This can save you 15.3% self-employment tax on the dividend portion, though S-Corp setup and administration costs must be factored in.
